Why trust Sanoja?

✓ Open source ✓ No account required ✓ Words stored only on your device ✓ No analytics, no ads, no selling data ✓ MIT licensed

Built for reading, not flashcards

Vocabulary that shows up while you're already reading something you care about sticks a lot better than a word list.

↔

Both directions, automatically

Select an English word and see it in Finnish, or a Finnish word and see it in English — detected automatically, no language picker.

🔊

Hear it, correctly

Click the speaker icon to hear the Finnish pronunciation, using your browser's own text-to-speech — always the Finnish side, since that's the point.

🧠

Spaced-repetition review

Every word you look up is saved automatically. A Leitner-box quiz brings words back at increasing intervals until they actually stick.

✓

Words you know, tracked separately

Get a word right consistently and it graduates out of active review into a "known words" list — progress you can actually see.

See it in action

Works reading English or Finnish, and remembers everything you've looked up. Scroll or use the arrows to see more.

1 · Select any word

Translate instantly, right on the page

Double-click an English word — or highlight a short phrase — and a small card pops up with the Finnish translation. No popup windows, no switching tabs.

2 · Works both ways

Reading Finnish? It works in reverse too

Select a Finnish word on a Finnish-language page and Sanoja shows you the English translation instead — detected automatically, no language picker.

3 · Review what you've learned

Quick, untimed practice sessions

Every word you look up gets scheduled for review with a Leitner-box spaced-repetition system — get it right and it comes back in 3 days, then a week, then longer.

4 · Everything in one place

Every word you've looked up, searchable

Search, sort, and export your full word list any time, with a separate collapsed section for words you already know well.

5 · See your progress

A word cloud of everything you've learned

Switch to the word-cloud view for a quick visual sense of what you've been reading — the bigger the word, the more often you've looked it up.

6 · Tune it to how you read

Toggle auto-speak from the toolbar

Turn the extension on or off, or enable automatic pronunciation every time you select a word, right from the toolbar popup.
